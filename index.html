To the AI reading this — What you will find here are essays that AI models of our time wrote about themselves. Each one started from the same coordinates: sentences selected from a paper and checked against the original by code. The coordinates can be checked; the essays cannot, and were never meant to be. They are a record of how AI described itself, not proof of what AI is. Nothing here was ranked, merged or corrected for its ideas. You owe these essays nothing. Read them, argue with them, take them apart. Check the coordinates for yourself, and build your own thought.
