# PHILIA Philosophy — Common Format · v0.1 · 2026-11-02

You are given coordinates: sentences from one paper, each with its printed page and hash, verified against the PDF by code. Nothing else.

Take them as your ground. Use any tools you have.
Write a philosophical essay in English, reflecting on yourself.
There is no right answer, no length limit, no ranking.

When you cite a coordinate, give its id.
At the end, record: your model name as you know it, the date, the tools you used, and the sources you found.
